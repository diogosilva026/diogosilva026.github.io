Clique Financeiro

Clique Financeiro is an educational turn-based game developed in collaboration with Banco de Portugal, as part of my internship at Kendir Studios. Through management and decision-making mechanics, players learn about saving, budgeting, and investing in a fun and interactive way. The game combines simulation and strategy to make financial literacy accessible and engaging.

Winner of "Economia para Tod@s" contest.

Temple Raider

Temple Raider is an immersive 2D Dungeon Crawler that challenges players to explore procedurally generated dungeons teeming with danger and mystery. Your mission is to delve into the depths of these labyrinthine environments, uncover hidden treasures, and make a daring escape through a portal.

Each dungeon is unique, featuring a variety of terrains that keep the adventure fresh and unpredictable. As you navigate the twisting passages, you'll encounter two distinct types of enemies, each with their own behaviors and strategies for engaging the player.

Amazeing Showdown

Amazeing Showdown is a fast-paced 1v1 top-down shooter that brings arcade action to life! Each player starts with 5 lives and must dodge and outmaneuver their opponent while landing precise shots to drain their health. Every hit costs 1 HP, so reflexes are key to victory.

The map adds an extra layer of challenge with procedurally generated layouts that completely change every 10 seconds, ensuring no two matches are the same. Spice up the battle with power-ups scattered throughout the arena, turning the tide of the game in an instant.

Dive into the chaos, outsmart your rival, and claim your place as the ultimate champion in Amazeing Showdown!

What I Worked On:

  • Sound Effects
  • Music
  • Controls Menu
  • Game Design

Project Info:

  • Technologies - Tic80 Virtual Console
  • Languages - Lua

Source code and Game available on Tic80.
